Privacy Policy — The Overland Trail

Last updated: September 7, 2026

The Overland Trail is a single-player wagon survival game. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle.

No account required

The game does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to play.

No data collection

The Overland Trail does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The game does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or any third-party tracking services.

Save data stays on your device

Your game progress is saved locally on your own device using your browser's or app's local storage. This save data never leaves your device, is never transmitted to us or to any third party, and is only accessible within the game itself.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other device data.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future update adds optional online features — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to chrisgoetz9@gmail.com.
